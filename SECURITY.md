# Security Policy

We take security bugs in Mini-ISP seriously. We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings, and will make every effort to acknowledge your contributions.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

To report a security issue, please use the GitHub Security Advisory "Report a Vulnerability" tab.

The Mini-ISP team will send a response indicating the next steps in handling your report. After the initial reply to your report, the security team will keep you informed of the progress towards a fix and full announcement, and may ask for additional information or guidance.
